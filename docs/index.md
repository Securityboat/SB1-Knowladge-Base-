## TriNetra Platform

<div class="grid cards trinetra-cards" markdown>

-   [:material-target: **Bug Bounty**](trinetra/bug-bounty.md)

    ---

    Crowd-sourced vulnerability discovery. Run public or private programs, triage
    submissions, and reward researchers for valid findings — all on one platform.

-   [:material-shield-check: **PTaaS**](trinetra/ptaas.md)

    ---

    Penetration Testing as a Service. Schedule, scope, and track penetration tests
    with verified security researchers. Real-time findings, remediation workflows,
    and compliance-ready reports.

-   [:material-robot-industrial: **Agentic Pentest**](trinetra/agentic-pentest.md)

    ---

    AI agents run recon and exploit-chaining around the clock. Nothing ships
    without a human signature. Continuous, autonomous testing with expert
    oversight.

-   [:material-radar: **Attack Surface Management**](trinetra/attack-surface-management.md)

    ---

    Continuous discovery and monitoring of your exposed surface. 11-stage scan
    pipeline across domains, IPs, and 15+ cloud providers. Know what attackers
    see, before they do.

-   [:material-shield-alert: **Digital Risk Protection**](trinetra/digital-risk-protection.md)

    ---

    Detect and takedown phishing clones, brand impersonations, and fraudulent
    domains. Side-by-side visual proof of detected threats with automated
    takedown workflows.
