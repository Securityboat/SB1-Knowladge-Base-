# Chat

The **Chat** tab provides a direct communication channel for the program.

---

## Channel Structure

Depending on the program's setup, the chat tab behaves in one of two ways:

### 1. Read-Only Channel (Standard)
For most public Bug Bounty programs, the chat channel is read-only. It is used by SecurityBoat triagers and client admins to post quick real-time alerts, scope announcements, or system downtime warnings.

### 2. Interactive Triage Chat (Private Programs)
In private, invite-only programs, the chat channel may be open for bidirectional conversation. Researchers can:
*   Ask questions regarding target scope or credentials.
*   Request clarification on specific policy terms.
*   Converse directly with the assigned SecurityBoat TPM.

---

## Chat Guidelines & Best Practices

If interactive chat is enabled, please adhere to these guidelines:
*   **Do Not Share Findings**: Do not paste vulnerability details, PoC code, or exploits in the general chat. Vulnerability reports must *only* be submitted via the **Submit Finding** drawer to ensure secure handling and track payout eligibility.
*   **Be Professional**: Maintain a polite and professional tone.
*   **No Spamming**: Keep questions concise and relevant to the scope of testing.
