# Security Researcher — Getting Started

> **Version 4.1 — August 2026** · Production guide for live researchers · en-GB

Welcome to the **Security Researcher** guide. As a researcher on Tri-Netra, you participate in penetration testing engagements and bug bounty programs to discover, document, and report security vulnerabilities.

---

## 1. Role & Responsibilities

- **Testing & Finding Submission:** Test assigned assets according to engagement scope, document reproducible PoCs, score vulnerabilities using CVSS v4.0, and submit findings for TPM verification.
- **Coverage Tracking:** Log methodology progress using the engagement coverage checklist.
- **Retesting:** Verify client remediations when assigned retest tasks.
- **Report Drafting (Lead Researchers):** Draft report narratives and executive summaries for review by the TPM.

---

## 2. Key Workflows

![Researcher dashboard — your home screen with engagements, findings, and KPIs.](../images/researcher_dashboard.png)

---

### Submitting a Finding
1. Open your assigned engagement from **Pentest Engagements**.
2. Click the **Findings** tab and select **Submit finding**.
3. Fill in the title, CVSS v4.0 vector, vulnerability type, affected endpoint, detailed description, business impact, remediation advice, and PoC evidence.
4. Save as **Draft** to continue editing, or click **Submit** to send to the TPM review queue.

### Managing Payouts
1. Go to **Settings → Payouts** to set up bank details and payout preferences.
2. Track earnings from verified findings and engagement fees in **Payouts**.
