## 12. AI Assistant

### 12.0 What it is for a researcher

The **AI Assistant** is a data-aware chat scoped to the data your role can access —
for a researcher, your engagements, your findings, and the opportunities/programs
you're part of. It's permission-scoped and respects the platform's visibility rules.

### Navigation

Click **AI Assistant** in the sidebar.

---

### 12.1 Using it

![Researcher AI Assistant — data-aware chat scoped to the researcher's work.](../images/res_ai_chat.png)

Ask in plain language, scoped to your work:

- "What findings have I submitted that are still unverified?"
- "Summarise the scope of my current engagement."
- "Which of my findings are eligible for payout?"
- "What's on the coverage checklist I haven't tested yet?"

Answers are grounded in your live, permission-scoped data; the conversation keeps
context for follow-ups.

### Best practices

- **Use it to keep track** of your submissions and coverage across engagements.
- **Click through to the module** (Findings, Engagements, Payouts) to act.
- **Don't paste client secrets or exploit payloads** into the chat.

### Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause / Fix |
|---------|-------------|
| **"I can't find that"** | The data is outside your engagements/scope. |
| **Looks stale** | Open the module directly — the live list is the source of truth. |
