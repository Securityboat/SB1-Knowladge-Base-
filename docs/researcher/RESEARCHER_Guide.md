# Researcher & Lead Researcher Guide

> **Version 4.0 — July 2026** · Production guide for live users · en-GB

This guide is for **Researchers** and **Lead Researchers** — the people who perform
the security testing: joining engagements, hunting vulnerabilities, and authoring
findings. Everything is scoped to **you** — your engagements, your findings, and
your pay.

## Chapters

| # | Chapter | What it covers |
|---|---------|----------------|
| 1 | [Introduction](01-introduction.md) | Your role, personal scope, and the modules you can access. |

| 3 | [Dashboard](03-dashboard.md) | Your work hub — active engagements, open findings, pay. |
| 4 | [Opportunities](04-opportunities.md) | Open engagement opportunities you can apply for. |
| 5 | [Invites](05-invites.md) | Direct invitations to join engagements. |
| 6 | [Engagements](06-engagements.md) | The engagements you're seated on — scope, team, timeline. |

| 9 | [Payouts](09-payouts.md) | Your earnings and payout history (your scope only). |
| 10 | [Identity Verification](10-verification.md) | Complete verification to unlock paid work. |
| 11 | [Bug Bounty](11-bug-bounty.md) | Programs you participate in — submit and track reports. |
| 12 | [AI Assistant](12-ai-assistant.md) | Ask about your engagements and findings. |

> **Not available to Researchers:** Clients, Assets, ASM, Reports, Compliance,

> everything above.
