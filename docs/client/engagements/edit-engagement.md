## Edit Engagement

While your engagement is in **Draft** state (after your request is approved but before testing preparation begins), **Client Admin** and **Client TPM** can refine scoping details to ensure the testing team has everything they need.

---

### 1. What you can update during Draft

| Detail | How to provide it |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Additional scope context** | Use the engagement **Chat** to share credentials, VPN access details, preferred testing hours, or updated URLs. |
| **Preferred schedule adjustments** | If your timeline has changed, communicate the new preferred dates via Chat so the SecurityBoat team can adjust planning. |
| **Asset scope corrections** | If the wrong asset was selected or the asset's scope has changed, update the asset under **Assets** first, then notify your TPM in Chat. |

> **Note:** client users don't have a direct "Edit" button on the engagement — detailed field editing is handled by your SecurityBoat TPM. However, any information you provide during the Draft stage (via Chat or by updating your asset records) will be incorporated before testing begins.

---

### 2. What you can see but not edit

Once the engagement moves past Draft into active preparation or Live testing, the following details are locked and managed by the SecurityBoat team:

- **Title and description** — set at request time.
- **Engagement type and testing approach** — confirmed during the scoping call.
- **Schedule** — finalized by SecurityBoat after the scoping call.
- **Rules of engagement** (in-scope / out-of-scope) — locked once testing begins.

You can always **view** all of these on the [Brief tab](engagement-details/engagement-detail.md) of the engagement detail page.

---

### 3. Communicating changes after Draft

If you need something changed after the engagement has moved past Draft:

1. **Open the engagement** and go to the **Chat** tab.
2. **Describe what needs to change** — be specific (e.g. "we've rotated the test credentials, here are the new ones" or "please exclude the /admin path — it's out of scope now").
3. Your **TPM** will review and apply the change on your behalf.

---

### Best practices

- **Provide all credentials and access details early** — ideally during Draft, before testing begins. Late access delivery delays the engagement.
- **Update your asset record** if URLs or IP ranges changed — the engagement's scope references your asset, so keeping the asset current keeps the engagement scope correct.
- **Use Chat, not email** — messages in engagement Chat are permanently attached to the engagement record and visible to the full SecurityBoat team working your pentest.

### Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause / Fix |
|---------|-------------|
| **I can't find a way to edit the engagement directly** | Client roles don't have a direct edit form — communicate changes to your TPM via Chat and they'll update the engagement on your behalf. |
| **My TPM hasn't applied my change** | Follow up in Chat. Changes communicated during active testing may take time to process depending on scope impact. |
| **The scope shown on Brief is outdated** | Update the asset under **Assets**, then notify your TPM so the engagement references are refreshed. |
