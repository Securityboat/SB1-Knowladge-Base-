    TriNetra — Agentic Security Platform

    Version 1.1 — August 2026 · Public product documentation

    TriNetra is SecurityBoat's agentic cybersecurity platform. It combines AI-driven automation with human expertise across thirteen integrated modules that cover the full security lifecycle — from continuous exposure discovery through offensive validation to risk governance.
